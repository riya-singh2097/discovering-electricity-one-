1. What is electricity?

Everything is made of atoms

Atoms have protons (+), electrons (−) and neutrons (no charge). Protons stay in the middle. Electrons can move.

Electricity = moving electrons

When electrons all flow the same way through a material, that flow is electricity. That is why it is called electr-icity!

A battery is a pump

A battery stores energy and pushes electrons round a path. Wires are the pipes. No path, no flow.

4. Activity 1: Your first circuit

A circuit is a closed path for electricity. It must go from the battery’s +, through the LED, and back to the battery’s −. If the path has a gap, it is an open circuit and nothing flows. If the path is complete, it is a closed circuit.

    Same circuit as water 💧

    How: tap one white dot, then another dot to join them with a wire. Tap a wire to remove it. Tap the switch lever to slide it on or off.
